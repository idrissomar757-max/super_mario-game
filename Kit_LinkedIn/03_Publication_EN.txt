Why revisit classic platform games? Because simple rules create demanding development challenges: precise controls, readable levels, and satisfying movement.

Here is my 2D platformer prototype, built with Unity and C# and inspired by the classics of the genre.

The project brings together a character controller with ground and air acceleration, variable jump height, coyote time, and jump buffering; a smooth camera with directional look-ahead; layered parallax backgrounds; collectible coins and scoring; patrolling enemies; and game-over and restart logic.

I also worked with a custom editor tool that generates the demonstration level and a transition scene, connecting gameplay programming with the development workflow.

For me, revisiting a traditional genre is a practical way to understand how movement, physics, camera behavior, and level layout shape the player experience.

I am looking to keep developing these skills through gameplay and prototyping opportunities with game development teams, and would welcome feedback on this project.

Visual assets: Kenney — Pixel Platformer; PROWNE — character assets via OpenGameArt. Work-in-progress portfolio prototype.
